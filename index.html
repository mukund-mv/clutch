About Us

We’re a student-led tech education startup created by learners, for learners. Our goal is simple: to make technology fun, easy to understand, and something you can actually use to create cool stuff.

At Synertech, we believe learning tech shouldn’t feel boring or complicated. That’s why we run workshops, projects, and sessions where you get to explore coding, robotics, AI, and more in a hands-on way. Think of it as learning by doing, not just listening.

What makes us different? We’re students too! We know the struggles, the confusion, and the excitement of discovering new skills. That’s why we keep things friendly, interactive, and always open to your ideas.

Synertech isn’t just about classes — it’s about building a community of creators and problem-solvers who want to use tech to make a difference. Whether you’re a beginner or already a tech geek, there’s a place for you here.
